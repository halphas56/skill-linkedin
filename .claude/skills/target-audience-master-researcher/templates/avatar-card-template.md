# Avatar Card — Template

**The output artefact of `../knowledge/avatar-from-comments.md`.** Also usable for avatars built from interviews, calls or reviews — the citation discipline is identical.

---

## The one rule

**No field may be filled without at least one verbatim citation.**

An empty field is a research task. An invented field is persona theatre with a source column. **An avatar with six sourced fields and eight blanks is a better deliverable than one with fourteen fields of confident invention** — because the blanks tell the client exactly what to find out next.

### Confidence codes

| Code | Requirement |
|---|---|
| `[E-high]` | ≥5 distinct people · ≥3 stimulus types · agreement signals present |
| `[E-med]` | ≥3 distinct people · ≥2 stimulus types |
| `[A]` | 1–2 people, or all from one stimulus → **hypothesis, label it as one** |
| `[blank]` | No evidence found → **leave empty, add to research tasks** |

## Contradiction log

Comment pairs that directly conflict. **Each unresolved contradiction is a second avatar hiding inside this one.**

| Claim A (verbatim) | Claim B (verbatim) | Same stimulus? | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| | | | split / same person in different moods / unresolved |

> **Never average a contradiction.** Averaging "too expensive" and "cheap for what it does" produces a fictional person who wants a moderately-priced product nobody asked for — the milkshake error.

## Limitations statement — **mandatory in the deliverable**

Copy this into the client-facing document. Do not omit it and do not soften it.

> This avatar is built from **___ public comments across ___ stimulus types and ___ platforms**, collected between ___ and ___.
>
> **It describes people who comment.** Commenters are a self-selected, vocal minority who skew toward the emotionally activated — delighted or annoyed. The quieter majority of buyers is not represented here.
>
> This method is strong evidence for **vocabulary, objections, current alternatives and awareness stage**. It is **not** evidence of prevalence: nothing here supports a claim that "X% of the audience thinks Y."
>
> Fields marked `[A]` are hypotheses from one or two comments and should be treated as research tasks, not findings. The blanks are listed above with the cheapest way to fill each.

## Delivery gate

- [ ] Every filled field has a verbatim citation
- [ ] Every field has a confidence code
- [ ] Blanks listed as ranked research tasks
- [ ] Circumstance section is **not empty**
- [ ] Contradiction scan run and logged
- [ ] Anti-avatar present
- [ ] Awareness mix counted, not estimated
- [ ] No inferred demographics
- [ ] No prevalence claims
- [ ] Verbatims exact — unedited, untranslated
- [ ] Commenters anonymised (platform + date, not names)
- [ ] Limitations statement included **verbatim**
- [ ] Avatar named by **circumstance**, not by a first name and an age
