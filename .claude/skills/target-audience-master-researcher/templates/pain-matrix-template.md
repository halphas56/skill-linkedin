# Pain Matrix & Pain Card — Template

**Methods:** `../knowledge/pain-discovery.md` (is it real?) · `../knowledge/pain-opportunity.md` (is it a business?)

**Two objects, two scores, deliberately not blended.**

> **⚠ Supersedes the single PPS.** The earlier version scored ten criteria into one number and mixed *how much this hurts* with *whether a business can be built on it*. A severe-but-unmonetisable pain and a mild-but-lucrative one landed on the same total. **Recompute old PPS totals; do not translate them.**

**And a reminder of the category error above this one:** the **TAS** ranks *segments*. Everything here ranks *pains* — within a segment, and across segments in Part 9.

## Part 3 — Classification: four axes

| # | Structural *(the response)* | Domain *(the language)* | Conversion *(will money move)* | Articulation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | outcome / obstacle / risk | functional · emotional · financial · social · time · operational · strategic · identity · risk · opportunity-cost · process · **control/uncertainty** · friction · **alternative** | compliance · time · risk · status · money · **none** | articulated / **latent** |

> **Structural drives the instrument:** outcome → fix · obstacle → **remove** · risk → **reassure**. You cannot reassure away an obstacle.
> **Conversion = "none"** is a finding, not a blank: real pain, no money.
> **Latent** means it will never be found by asking about problems. Only by observing behaviour.

**Fields "our mechanism" and "proof" are a hard gate.** Naming a pain you cannot relieve, or cannot prove you relieve, is **threat without efficacy** — EPPM predicts defensive avoidance, denial and reactance. ## Part 9 — Pain × Segment matrix

**Where pain research feeds back into audience research.** Score Pain Strength per cell.

| Pain | Segment A | Segment B | Segment C |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| | | | |
| | | | |

**Read the rows** for concentration and thresholds — a row rising sharply has found an axis along which the pain scales.
**Read the columns** for positioning — a standout value tells you what to lead with *for that segment*.
**Read flat rows sceptically** — either genuinely universal (table stakes, cannot differentiate) or scored on complaints rather than behaviour. Check the rung.

> **The segmentation test derived from pain data:** if one pain behaves completely differently across two segments, they were correctly separated. **If every pain scores identically across two segments, they are one segment.**

## Part 10 — Ethical review

**Per message, not per campaign.**

```
THREAT raised:      ______________________________________
EFFICACY offered:   ______________________________________
  Response efficacy — "the fix works":  ☐ stated ☐ proven ☐ absent
  Self-efficacy — "YOU can do this":    ☐ stated ☐ proven ☐ absent
                                          ↑ the half most often skipped
Distance between threat and efficacy on the page: ______________
  ⚠ Relief below the fold = fear control by LAYOUT, whatever the intent.
```

| Autonomy condition | Preserved? | Violated by |
|---|---|---|
| **Full information** | ☐ | Hidden price, hidden effort, no "who this isn't for" |
| **Emotional space** | ☐ | Fake countdowns, "only 3 left", pressure framing |
| **A clear exit** | ☐ | Hidden cancellation, dark-pattern opt-out, guilt on refusal |

**Prohibited — any one present, it does not ship:**
☐ Amplifying a pain they hadn't felt ☐ Fake urgency ☐ Shame framing
☐ Catastrophising ☐ Vague mechanism ☐ Obscured exit

**Three self-check questions — answer in writing:**
```
1 Would I say this to their face, knowing they'd read the fine print?
2 If they act on this and it doesn't work out — informed, or fooled?
3 Is the efficacy claim in the same breath, or three scrolls away?
```

## Part 11 — Delivery gate

- [ ] Every pain **verbatim**; every quotation mark traces to a source and date
- [ ] Latent pains sought by observing behaviour, not by asking about problems
- [ ] **Both chain directions** run — laddering up, pain chain down
- [ ] Seven links labelled `confirmed / inferred / hypothesis`
- [ ] Branching check run; divergent roots **split**
- [ ] All **four axes** classified
- [ ] Differential diagnosis run; drops recorded
- [ ] **Behavioural rung** recorded; workaround detail captured where B3+
- [ ] Six validation tests; test 6 explicitly checked
- [ ] **Trigger** established — or "pain, not buyer" stated plainly
- [ ] **Evidence AGAINST populated** — *"searched, none found"* if that is the truth
- [ ] **Cost of inaction** with arithmetic shown and multipliers sourced
- [ ] **Two scores**, no blended total anywhere
- [ ] Quadrant named — including **Charity** where honest
- [ ] Gates checked separately
- [ ] Concentration · threshold · evolution examined
- [ ] Pain × Segment matrix built where >1 segment is live
- [ ] Mechanism + proof present for anything used in acquisition
- [ ] EPPM balance and autonomy conditions checked per message
- [ ] **At least one pain declined as non-commercial**, with the reason

> **The test:** name the pain that hurts most and is worth least. If you cannot, you measured only one thing.
